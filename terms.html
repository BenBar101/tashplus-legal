The service

TASH+ lists local businesses and their deals for school students, university students, soldiers and discharged soldiers. TASH+ does not sell the listed goods or process payments. Businesses are responsible for their deals and for honoring them.

Eligibility

The deal groups you choose only decide which deals you see. The business decides on site whether you qualify, for example by asking for a student card or military ID. You must be at least 15 to use TASH+, and you must give your real date of birth.

Using a deal

To use a deal, show your TASH+ QR code at the business. The business scans it, sees your name, age and chosen deal groups, and records the deal you used. Each deal can be used once per day. Do not share, copy or reuse another person's code.
